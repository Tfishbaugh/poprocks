# Credits

## Sounds

Most PopRocks sounds are synthesized in the browser. Four short samples come from Freesound. All four are CC0, so no credit is required, but here it is anyway, and in the app under Sounds:

- deleted_user_5959249: the guitar chord (Rock's Chord)
- gusgus26: the guitar note (Rock's Riff)
- JoMungus: the crash cymbal (Rock's Crash)
- khenshom: the "Hey!" shout (K-pop's Hey!)

LICENSES.md lists each file's source and what was changed.

A sample under CC BY 3.0 or 4.0 would also have to be credited here and in the app's "Sounds by…" line. tests/unit/kits.test.js checks both.
