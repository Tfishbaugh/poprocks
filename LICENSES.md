# Licenses

PopRocks' code, styles and icons were made for this app. The only outside content is four short sound samples in `sounds/`, listed below. tests/unit/kits.test.js checks that every file in `sounds/` is listed here with an allowed license.

## Sound samples

Rules for samples: CC0 1.0 first; CC BY 3.0 or 4.0 only when no CC0 sound fits, credited in CREDITS.md and in the app. Never NC, ND, SA, custom or unknown licenses. Every sample comes from Freesound, under the license on its page, and the uploader is the author.
